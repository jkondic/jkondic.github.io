Jovana Kondic

I'm a PhD student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at MIT, advised by Prof. Aude Oliva in the Computational Perception and Cognition Lab. My research focuses on vision-language models, chart understanding, and synthetic data generation.

Previously, I completed my MS at MIT, where I worked on Monte Carlo methods for motion planning and goal inference, and my BSE at Princeton in Electrical Engineering with certificates in Cognitive Science, Robotics, and ML & Statistics. I've interned at IBM Research and done research at Stanford.
